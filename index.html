Overview

Programmable 3D worlds from video

WRAP transforms videos into programmable 3D worlds, enabling multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to reason about and manipulate 3D scenes.

By grounding visual objects into persistent 3D variables, WRAP allows MLLMs to dynamically generate and execute programs for flexible geometric computations, supporting both video spatial reasoning and 3D scene editing.

 for(int f=0;f<count;f++) {

    Method

    From video to executable 3D programs

    WRAP gives a multimodal language model a way to refer to objects it sees and compute directly on their 3D geometry.

    OBJECT_1

    Visual-Geometric Entity Binding

    WRAP builds object point clouds from video and links them to persistent visual IDs. Each ID resolves to a Grounded Variable.

    Clean video Tagged video

    Adaptive 3D Activation

    The model starts with untagged video and switches to tagged frames when it needs explicit 3D computation.

    3D Program Reasoning

    The model selects the relevant variables and writes a task-specific program. The executor runs it to answer the question or update the scene.
